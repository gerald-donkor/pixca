# 72 — Side-by-Side Perspective Comparison View

## Goal

Provide readers on the **News Details Page** ([`app/article/[id]/page.tsx`](file:///home/dg/Projects/nextjs/pixca/app/article/[id]/page.tsx)) and within the **Related Articles** section ([`components/ui/related-articles.tsx`](file:///home/dg/Projects/nextjs/pixca/components/ui/related-articles.tsx)) with an interactive **"Compare Perspectives"** experience:
1. **Interactive Comparison Trigger**: Enhance related article cards with a "Compare" action badge and subtle divergence indicator highlighting how the related article's political framing or sentiment contrasts with the active article.
2. **Perspective Comparison Modal** ([`components/ui/perspective-comparison-modal.tsx`](file:///home/dg/Projects/nextjs/pixca/components/ui/perspective-comparison-modal.tsx)): An accessible, responsive modal comparing the two articles side-by-side:
   - **Perspective Delta Header**: Shows the divergence in framing (e.g. "+35% Left Lean", "+40% Right Lean", or "Framing Divergence: High/Moderate").
   - **Side-by-Side Spectrum Meters**: Visualizes both articles' Left / Center / Right percentage distributions with color coding.
   - **Sentiment & Tone Comparison**: Displays side-by-side sentiment labels, scores, and confidence ratings.
   - **Summary & Framing Notes Contrast**: Displays neutral AI summaries, framing notes, and extracted loaded terms for both articles.
   - **Quick Actions**: "Read Full Article", "Swap Comparison", "Copy Comparison Link", and responsive tabbed switching on mobile viewports.
3. **GSAP Micro-Interactions**: Smooth modal entry/exit transitions, staggered comparison row reveals, and interactive hover bounce effects.

## Decisions and assumptions

1. **Client-Driven Perspective Comparison**:
   - The news details page already loads the primary article with its full analysis (`summary`, `framing_notes`, `loaded_terms`, `bias_label`, percentages, etc.) and related articles via `getRelatedArticles`.
   - We pass the primary article analysis to `RelatedArticles`, transforming it into an interactive client component that can open the comparison modal with any selected related article without requiring extra network requests for basic comparison metrics.
2. **API Endpoint for Extended Related Details (Optional / Lazy Fetch)**:
   - For related articles whose full summary or framing notes are not included in `RelatedArticleRow`, provide an optional lightweight fetch via `GET /api/article/[id]` or display the available `RelatedArticleRow` metrics cleanly with an instant transition.
3. **Responsive Dual-Column vs. Tabbed Layout**:
   - On desktop screens (`md:` / `lg:`), render a side-by-side split comparison view.
   - On mobile screens (`< md`), render an ergonomic tab switcher (`Article A` vs `Article B` vs `Delta Summary`) to prevent horizontal cramped layout.
4. **GSAP Animation**:
   - Animate the comparison modal container and metric delta bars using `@gsap/react` `useGSAP()` with `transform` and `autoAlpha` for 60fps performance.

## Implementation requirements

1. **Create `components/ui/perspective-comparison-modal.tsx`**:
   - Props: `open: boolean`, `onOpenChange: (open: boolean) => void`, `primaryArticle: PrimaryArticleComparisonData`, `targetArticle: RelatedArticleRow | null`.
   - Render Base UI Dialog with:
     - Header: "Perspective Comparison" + Perspective Divergence Badge (e.g. "Contrasting Perspectives: Left vs. Right" or "Consensus Framing").
     - Top Summary Banner: Shows side-by-side source names, publish dates, and overall lean delta.
     - Dual-Column Comparison Grid (Desktop) / Tab Switcher (Mobile):
       - Column 1: Primary Article (Title, Source, Bias Meter, Sentiment, Summary, Loaded Terms).
       - Column 2: Target Related Article (Title, Source, Bias Meter, Sentiment, Link to full article).
     - Visual Comparison Highlights:
       - Delta badge calculating absolute difference in left/right lean: `Math.abs((primary.left_percentage - primary.right_percentage) - (target.left_percentage - target.right_percentage)) / 2`.
       - Highlighting contrasting sentiment (e.g., Negative vs. Positive).
     - Action Footer: "Read Compared Article", "Close", "Copy Comparison Link".
2. **Update `components/ui/related-articles.tsx`**:
   - Mark `"use client"`.
   - Accept `currentArticle?: PrimaryArticleComparisonData` alongside `articles: RelatedArticleRow[]`.
   - For each related article card, render a subtle "Compare" action badge / button overlaying or beside the card.
   - Wire click handler to open `PerspectiveComparisonModal` without interfering with navigation when clicking the main link.
3. **Update `app/article/[id]/page.tsx`**:
   - Pass `currentArticle` props (id, title, sourceName, image_url, published_at, analysis details) to `<RelatedArticles />`.
